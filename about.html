Our Story

New shop. Decades of finishing experience.

Kentucky Powder Coating was built by people who care about doing the work the right way — with proper pretreatment, careful application, and finishes that hold up to the conditions they were specified for. No shortcuts on the steps that matter.

We partnered with the team at Rangeris to bring that same commitment to quality and accountability into a new finishing operation serving Kentucky and the surrounding region.

Capabilities

The equipment behind the finish.

Large-Part Capacity

We're built for the heavy stuff — capacity for structural steel, long weldments, and oversized fabrications up to 40 feet long that most shops can't take.

Surface Preparation

Media blasting, degreasing, and chemical pretreatment to remove rust, mill scale, oils, and contaminants — the foundation of a coating that actually bonds and lasts.

Powder Application & Cure

Electrostatic powder application color-matched to your sample, with masking for threaded or sealing surfaces, then cured to the powder manufacturer's specification for full hardness, adhesion, and chemical resistance.

Quality Control

Every job is inspected for coverage, film build, and adhesion before it leaves the shop. Spec-critical work? Tell us your requirements up front and we'll build to them.
