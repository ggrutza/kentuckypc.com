About Us

A Kentucky finishing shop, built for real industrial work.

Kentucky Powder Coating was founded to bring industrial-grade finishing closer to home for the fabricators, builders, and makers of the Commonwealth.

Our Story

New shop. Decades of finishing experience.

Kentucky Powder Coating was built by operators who came up on shop floors, running blast cabinets, tuning spray guns, and chasing bake-off defects long before they ran the business. We started KPC to do the work the right way — with proper pretreatment, documented QC, and finishes that hold up to the conditions they were specified for.

We partnered with the team at Rangeris to bring that same commitment to quality and accountability into a new finishing operation serving Kentucky and the surrounding region.

Capabilities

The equipment behind the finish.

Surface Preparation

Enclosed blast bay with steel grit and aluminum oxide media, hot-wash degreasing, and multi-stage iron-phosphate pretreatment for ferrous substrates.

Application Booth

Controlled-environment powder booth with automated reclaim, certified applicators, and color change capability for short-run production.

Cure Oven

Large-format batch oven with capacity for parts up to 40 feet long and 10 feet wide, and digital temperature logging per batch for documented cure verification.

Quality Control

Dry film thickness, cross-hatch adhesion, and mil-gauge inspection. Documented QC reports available on request for spec-critical jobs.
