Our Process

Four stages. Zero shortcuts.

A coating only lasts if the steps behind it are done right. Here's how every job runs, from your first call to parts ready for pickup.

  • 01Consult & estimate. Send parts, specs, and timeline — we return an itemized quote, usually within a business day.
  • 02Prep & pretreatment. Blasting, degreasing, and chemical pretreatment so the coating actually bonds.
  • 03Electrostatic application. Color-matched powder, with masking for threaded and sealing surfaces.
  • 04Cure & inspect. Cured to spec in the batch oven, checked for coverage and adhesion, then packaged.
