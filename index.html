Our Process

Four stages. Zero shortcuts.

A coating only lasts if the steps behind it are done right. Here's how every job runs, from your first call to parts ready for pickup.

  • 01Consult & estimate. Send parts, specs, and timeline — we return an itemized quote, usually within a business day.
  • 02Prep & pretreatment. Blasting, degreasing, and chemical pretreatment so the coating actually bonds.
  • 03Electrostatic application. Color-matched powder, with masking for threaded and sealing surfaces.
  • 04Cure & inspect. Cured to spec in the batch oven, checked for coverage and adhesion, then packaged.

Common Questions

Good to know.

What can you powder coat?

Most metals — mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and cast iron. We coat industrial, automotive, architectural, agricultural, and custom parts, from one-off pieces to production runs.

How large a part can you coat?

We handle large, heavy parts up to 40 feet long — including structural steel, long weldments, trailers, and oversized fabrications that won't fit at most shops. Send us the dimensions and we'll confirm.

Where are you located and what areas do you serve?

We're at 679 Parker Drive, Maysville, KY 41056, serving Maysville, Mason County, northeastern Kentucky, and the surrounding region.

What colors and finishes are available?

Hundreds of RAL colors in gloss, satin, matte, textured, metallic, and candy/translucent finishes. If you have a RAL code or a sample to match, bring it.

How long does it take, and how do I get a quote?

We respond to most quote requests within one business day; turnaround depends on size, quantity, and prep. Use the Request a Quote form or email info@kentuckypc.com with your parts, quantity, size, substrate, and color.

Do you do surface prep and small jobs?

Yes. We provide in-house media blasting, degreasing, and chemical pretreatment, and we take both one-off custom pieces and larger production runs.
