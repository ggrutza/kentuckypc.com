Our Process

Four stages. Zero shortcuts.

01

Consult & Estimate

Share your parts, specs, and timeline. We'll recommend the right coating system and return a detailed, itemized quote — usually within one business day.

02

Prep & Pretreatment

Media blasting, degreasing, and chemical pretreatment remove rust, mill scale, oils, and contaminants so the coating bonds cleanly to the substrate.

03

Application

Electrostatic powder application in a controlled environment. Color-matched to sample, with full masking for threaded, sealing, or finished surfaces.

04

Cure & QC

Parts are cured in our industrial batch oven, inspected for film build and adhesion, then packaged for pickup or delivery.
